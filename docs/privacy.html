Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the RePixIV Android app handles information. RePixIV is an independent third-party client, not affiliated with Pixiv Inc., Google LLC or YouTube.

Summary: RePixIV stores app settings, Pixiv authorization tokens, favorite tags, hidden-post preferences, music favorites and music play history in app-private storage on your device. When you use Pixiv or the optional Player features, the app communicates with the relevant Pixiv or Google/YouTube services. The project documentation does not describe a RePixIV-operated account server or analytics backend.

Information handled

  • Pixiv: Signing in may provide account/profile information and authorization tokens needed for feeds, bookmarks, likes, comments and following. The app also supports manually entering a Pixiv refresh token.
  • Local data: Settings, Pixiv tokens, favorite tags, not-interested post preferences, music favorites and music play history are stored in app-private Android storage. Android backup is disabled for app data.
  • Downloads: Artwork you choose to download is saved on-device in Pictures/RePixIV.
  • Google/YouTube: If you use Google sign-in in the Player, RePixIV uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE through your device browser and requests YouTube read-only access for music discovery/search and related account features. Playback uses YouTube's embedded player.

Use of information

Information is used to provide features you request: loading Pixiv content, performing account actions, saving preferences, searching music and playing selected YouTube videos. The project does not sell personal information. RePixIV does not ask for your Google or Pixiv account password; sign-in is handled through the relevant provider's authentication flow.

Third-party services

Your device communicates with Pixiv and Google/YouTube when you use those features. YouTube's embedded player may also communicate with YouTube. These providers process information under their own policies. RePixIV uses Pixiv's unofficial mobile API, which may change without notice. See Google's Privacy Policy, YouTube's Terms and Pixiv's terms.

Sharing, retention and deletion

The project documentation describes local storage rather than a RePixIV-operated cloud account or central activity database. Data is sent to the relevant provider when necessary for a feature you initiate. Local app data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. Downloaded artwork remains until you delete it. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account settings and manage Pixiv sessions through Pixiv. Clearing RePixIV data does not delete information held by Pixiv, Google or YouTube.

Children's privacy

RePixIV is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Pixiv content may include mature material; follow applicable age requirements and local law.

Security

The project documentation states that tokens are stored in app-private SharedPreferences and Android backup is disabled. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device protected and never publish credentials or private tokens.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, contact the maintainer through RePixIV GitHub Issues.
